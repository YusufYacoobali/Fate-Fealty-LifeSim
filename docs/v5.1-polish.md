# Fate & Fealty v5.1 — gameplay and UI polish

Implemented on `v5`, starting from reviewed commit `58a5c441e63b5c262688ecafa70eb1c4c9d1450d`. Verification date: 30 October 2026.

## Gameplay changes

- **Tuition support:** charity, merit, household funding and named patrons have separate requirements. Applications consider poverty, learning, conduct, institutional support and relationships, can fail, and explain when to retry. Household and patron awards spend their actual resources. New awards cover one route’s tuition once, including awards from event effects. Existing v6 scholarship benefits are honoured through an explicit legacy award.
- **Named narration:** both `{{client}}` and `{{client.name}}` resolve the saved actor. The same renderer covers passage text, choice labels, feed outcomes and permanent history. Household lessons credit the living carer shown in the encounter rather than an unrelated or deceased contact.
- **Adult learning:** the 24 childhood lessons end at seventeen. Twelve adult encounters cover independent study, literacy, late apprenticeship, scholarly/religious training, professional martial training and private tutoring. Adult classmates are adults; earlier friendships, mentors and grudges remain recorded. Borrowed certificates can face a later review; legitimately earned qualifications survive that review.
- **Families:** seeded parent names, ages, occupations, personalities, ambitions and bonds vary. Most households remain ordinary. Rarer circumstances include struggling merchants, prosperous workshops, indebted nobles, disgraced service, surviving parents, relative guardians and a divided palace. These change funding, contacts, resources and political support. Relative guardians are actual aunts or uncles in the genealogy, sharing a recorded parent with the surviving mother or father. Parent-child links are reciprocal, generation ages are plausible, and existing romance protections recognise the relationship. These links are generated only for new births. Fourteen additional origin encounters affect actual household resources and named contacts. Two early household encounters prevent otherwise empty preschool stretches.
- **Professional decisions:** all thirty base activities now open the existing EventModal to choose a method. Skills, relevant qualifications, experience, reputation, client trust/personality, competition and world conditions affect success. Setbacks consume preparation costs and grant smaller learning rewards; successful work earns its own milestones. Shared shipments, commissions, apprentices, parish work, company duties and patronage retain distinct effects.
- **Choosing professional clients:** medicine and trade decisions offer the existing filtered, searchable person picker inside EventModal. Actual sick children and adults can be selected; preventive consultations remain available when nobody is ill. Trading counterparties must be eligible adults. Changing the selected person saves their actor ID without another fee, action, relationship reward or RNG draw. The selected trader owns the actual payment.
- **Medical and legal correctness:** diagnosis selects existing illness/injury or gives a preventive consultation; it never creates illness. Care can improve an actual illness or injury or fail, with harmful interventions producing patient complaints, legal evidence and lasting grievances in an injured noble family. Supportive care can ease pain without removing a permanent disability. A second opinion pays an actual qualified adult practitioner; a suitable physician is retained if none is available. Advocates prepare evidence; they cannot pardon prisoners, erase genuine charges or terminate sentences. Recorded royal pardons remain a ruler’s explicit judicial decision.
- **Clinical follow-ups:** saved patient records compare the original condition and health with the actual later simulation. New illness or a serious health decline prevents a false full-recovery claim; a death is recorded without automatically inventing malpractice. Reviews are limited to two per year, including across reloads. The records remain after changing careers and pass to an heir, but an heir earns recovery credit only for their own care episode.
- **Professional consequences:** the twelve authored stories remain, with four branches capable of immediate resolution and other branches returning for a later reckoning. Fraud can create named complaints, family hostility and retained evidence. Rivals can undercut commissions, contest influence or expose recorded misconduct, with a minimum three-year interval and at most one rival move in the annual feed.
- **Contested court:** petitioning opens a saved hearing while the incumbent keeps the seal. Learning, service, influence, ruler trust, vacancies, incumbent competence, council supporters, a named rival and actual patron support affect the decision. Outcomes include appointment, rejection/retention, conditional further service and withdrawal. Changed rulers or officeholders invalidate stale petitions rather than being overwritten.
- **Council and opposition:** councillors give advice based on their office, interests and current conditions. Their reactions use the advice given *before* the decree: successful mobilisation cannot turn an endorsed order into retrospective dissent. Ignored grievances form persistent coalitions that can withhold cooperation, back claimants, demand an audience and contribute to a rebellion. Tax resistance, rejected embassies, competence in command, pardon grievances and jealous title rivals provide contextual consequences; stated base costs remain predictable.

The four primary stats, social estates, knighthood, crafting, caravans, RPG encounters, legacy systems and Premium boundaries remain in place. Court office is separate from social rank. All player monarch routes still require Premium, and historical genealogy and former titles survive entitlement changes.

## Save compatibility

Save and content versions advance to **7** while retaining the `mls:world:v5` storage key and additive support for older envelopes. Migration consumes no RNG, preserves saved names and professional/learning records, and retains future-save protection. The new clinical/adviser fields are optional additions to schema 7. Old saves do not gain invented ancestors or changed names. A saved childhood lesson accidentally served to an adult recovers to that route’s adult encounter with an adult peer. Unfinished professional work and court conflicts survive normal reload and heir continuation.

## Review corrections — 31 October 2026

- Alliance actions and the person picker share one eligibility check. Both households must exist and be distinct, the representative must be a living adult, and neither household may already record the alliance. Invalid requests earn no influence and spend no action, council allowance or RNG. A successful agreement records both sides and awards influence once.
- A critical player reign opens a saved opposition crisis before any annual deposition. The crisis bypasses the usual audience cooldown and remains unanswered when event selection, another trial or a reload delays the encounter. Only a valid opposition choice records the response. Deposition can follow on a later annual update if legitimacy, public order and coalition strength remain critical; negotiation or recovery can avert it. Changed rulers clear the previous ruler’s crisis, and NPC coups continue normally.
- The People tab uses **KIN** when its measured navigation width and system text scale leave insufficient space for **PEOPLE**. The full accessibility label and tab destination remain People. See [280px with 35% larger text](validation/v5.1/people-large-text-280.png).
