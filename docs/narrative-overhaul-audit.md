## Findings and coverage plan

- Annual narration is the largest repetition bottleneck: 38 lines, a six-year exact-ID cooldown, a recent-feed window of only eight entries, no theme ledger, and eligibility inferred from literal capitalised parent names. A dying character can also be killed by unattended flavour damage. Rewrite weak lines, expand to 160, resolve actual carers/spouses by canonical identity, require relevant world/career conditions and use a twelve-year message cooldown plus theme spacing. Flavour must not make irreversible life decisions.
- Older village/court banks repeatedly fall back on turnips, geese, relic vendors and vague reputation/stat rewards. Replace the weakest situations inside their stable IDs, rewrite the remaining weak passages, and give named antagonists, concrete household/legal effects and tempting trade-offs to the premises that merit them.
- The newer education, profession, origin, possession and persistent story banks contain stronger distinct writing. Preserve their identity. Review actor/state consistency rather than adding jokes to every serious passage. Specific overclaims in betrothal, apprentice and tithe chains need narrower narration or actual state changes.
- The 3–6-year chain gap makes a five-beat practical dispute consume decades. Use explicit shorter timing on selected urgent household, illness and local dispute chains; preserve existing saved due dates and keep long-running political/family stories where time is justified.
- Theme groups currently default to each event ID, so several relic/tax/goose premises evade thematic cooldowns. Share groups for overlapping independent premises and keep continuity exempt from thematic suppression. Improve weighting using actual eligibility/context, not free-floating generic replacements.
- Existing outcome.next arcs lose actor bindings. Conditional optional callbacks should use the existing world history/choice records with saved actor IDs, retaining named people across reloads and avoiding a new consequences database.
- Add 130 independent encounters in eight reviewed category batches (20 family, 18 romance, 20 crime, 20 work, 20 politics, 12 faith, 12 health/danger, 8 rare). Add 48 optional short callback opportunities to new and existing decisions. Verify 30–40 high-stakes scenarios through actual health, household, employment, marriage or legal state. Preserve free/Premium access.
- Reproducible before/after simulations will report available and encountered content, exact-ID/theme repeats, callback timing, serious effects, story completion and quiet stretches across all seven life roles. Read sample histories for comic device overuse, warmth and coherence. No UI redesign is planned.
